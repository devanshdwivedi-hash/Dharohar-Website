Ancient Indian Knowledge Systems RPG

DHAROHAR

A Journey Through Indian Heritage

Dharohar 1.0 is an immersive 2D educational RPG built with Godot Engine 4.x, designed to celebrate and preserve ancient Indian knowledge systems, culture, and heritage through interactive gameplay, domain-specific cutscenes, historical knowledge quizzes, and intellectual puzzle.

Our Core Philosophy

The 5-Stage Learning Loop

How Dharohar translates ancient knowledge traditions into engaging modern gameplay.

Step 01

Explore

Walk historically inspired 2D pixel-art recreations of ancient Nalanda stupas, viharas, and courtyards.

Step 02

Discover

Examine ancient palm-leaf manuscripts, structural monuments, and historical artefacts via building prompts.

Step 03

Learn

Engage in rich dialogue with historic scholars like Teacher Silabhadra and itinerant traders.

Step 04

Think

Contemplate foundational scientific theories and philosophical dilemmas from the Nyaya and Vedic schools.

Step 05

Solve

Apply acquired knowledge through interactive domain puzzles to earn intellectual passage.
